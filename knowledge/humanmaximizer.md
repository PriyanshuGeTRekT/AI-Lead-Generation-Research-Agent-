# Human Maximizer

> Human Maximizer is an all-in-one HRMS for Indian businesses — covering hiring, payroll, attendance, leave, performance, communication, and exit — plus Productivity Lens for real-time workforce visibility. Built for teams of 10 to 500.

Human Maximizer is built by Razor Infotech (New Delhi, India). Pricing ranges from ₹490/month (Ignite, 10 employees) to ₹5,598/month (Apex, 50 employees), with a ₹2,40,000/5-year Legacy plan. Productivity Lens is exclusive to Apex.

## Core HR Features

- [Core HR](https://humanmaximizer.com/core-hr): Employee records, KYE, document management, reminders, HR reports, and company policy workflows.
- [Employee Data Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/employee-data-management): Centralised employee records with real-time analytics for faster HR operations.
- [Organization Chart](https://humanmaximizer.com/organization-chart): Visualise roles, departments, and reporting structures in one dashboard.
- [Employee Self Onboarding](https://humanmaximizer.com/employee-self-onboarding): Digital onboarding for document collection, profile completion, and policy acknowledgement.
- [Employment Docs](https://humanmaximizer.com/employment-docs): Secure digital storage and automation for employment documents.

## Time & Attendance

- [Attendance Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/attendance-management): Track attendance via geo-location, biometric, and mobile app. Shift management, overtime, and policy configuration.
- [Leave Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/leave-management): Configurable leave policies, balance tracking, and approval workflows.
- [Geo-Fencing](https://humanmaximizer.com/geo-fencing): Location-based attendance, live tracking, and trip history for field teams. Prevents proxy punching.
- [Roster Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/roster-management): Build shift schedules, swap shifts, and send automated alerts.

## Payroll & Compliance

- [Payroll](https://humanmaximizer.com/payroll): Automated payroll processing with TDS, PF, ESIC, PT compliance, payslip generation, and direct bank transfer (PayNow). Available on all plans.
- [Resignation & FNF](https://humanmaximizer.com/resignation-and-fnf): Paperless offboarding with automated Full & Final settlement. Available on Pulse, Pulse Plus & Apex.

## Productivity & Performance

- [Productivity Lens](https://humanmaximizer.com/productivity-lens): Real-time workforce productivity tracking — app/website usage, break patterns, activity logs, analytics dashboards. Apex plan only.
- [Employee Performance Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/employee-performance-management): Performance reviews, goal setting, and feedback. Available on Pulse Plus & Apex.
- [Task Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/task-management): Assign daily and recurring tasks, checklists, status tracking, and alerts.
- [Recruitment](https://humanmaximizer.com/recruitment): Hiring pipelines, job requisition workflows, internal postings, and referrals.

## Collaboration & Support

- [Synergy](https://humanmaximizer.com/synergy): Internal social platform with polls, surveys, messaging, and group communication. Available on Pulse Plus & Apex.
- [Team Chats](https://humanmaximizer.com/chats): Built-in team chat connected to HR tasks, requests, and approvals. Available on Pulse Plus & Apex.
- [Ticket Management](https://humanmaximizer.com/ticket-management): Centralised ticketing with SLA tracking, auto-routing, and resolution analytics. Available on Pulse, Pulse Plus & Apex.
